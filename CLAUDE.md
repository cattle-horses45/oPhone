# oPhone Store

## Design Context

- **[PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)** — Strategic design direction. Register: hybrid (品牌 user-facing, 产品 admin). Brand personality: 科技 · 极简 · 冷静. Anti-references: SaaS 模板美学, 促销弹窗, 旧暗黑+金色主题. Accessibility: WCAG AA.
- **[DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md)** — Visual design system (seed). Creative North Star: "The Silent Gallery". Cold-tone dominant palette, sans body + mono labels, flat-by-default elevation, responsive motion. ⚠️ SEED — re-run `/impeccable document` after implementing new design tokens.

### Quick Reference (from PRODUCT.md)
1. **Let the product speak** — 产品 > 设计
2. **Breathing room is a feature** — 大留白是功能
3. **Consistency builds trust** — 统一视觉语言
4. **Efficiency without coldness** — 管理后台也要好看
5. **Every pixel earns its place** — 无纯装饰元素

### Visual Rules (from DESIGN.md)
- **The 10% Rule** — 强调色 ≤10% 面积
- **The Cold Canvas Rule** — 背景必须带冷色调
- **The Two-Typeface Rule** — 只用无衬线 + 等宽
- **The Flat-By-Default Rule** — 阴影只在交互状态出现
